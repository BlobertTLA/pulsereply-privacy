PulseReply Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

PulseReply (“we”, “our”, or “the Service”) helps users automatically reply to Instagram comments using rules they configure. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Instagram account information you authorize through Meta/Instagram Login, such as your Instagram user ID, username, account type, and permissions granted to the app.
  • Access tokens needed to call Instagram APIs on your behalf (for example, to read comments and post replies).
  • Comment and media metadata related to posts on the connected Instagram account, including comment text, comment IDs, usernames, and media IDs, solely to match rules and send replies.
  • Rule and activity data you create in the dashboard, such as keyword rules, reply templates, and logs of replies or skipped comments.
  • Technical data such as server logs needed to operate and debug the Service.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Connect and authenticate your Instagram professional account
  • Receive or fetch comments on your Instagram media
  • Match comments against rules you configure
  • Post comment replies you authorize
  • Show activity history in your dashboard
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Service

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Instagram data to train unrelated machine-learning models or for advertising.

3. Instagram / Meta platform data

When you connect Instagram, Meta provides data according to the permissions you approve (such as instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_manage_comments). We only request permissions required for comment automation and only use platform data to provide that functionality.

You can disconnect Instagram at any time from the PulseReply dashboard or revoke access in your Instagram/Meta account settings.

4. Storage and retention

Connected account credentials, rules, and activity logs are stored so the Service can operate. We retain this information while your account remains connected or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion by disconnecting your account or contacting us.

5. Sharing of information

We may share information only with:

  • Meta/Instagram, as required to authenticate and perform API actions you request
  • Infrastructure providers that host the Service
  • Authorities, if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the Service

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect tokens and account data, including storing secrets outside client-side code and limiting access to operational systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices

  • Disconnect Instagram from the dashboard
  • Revoke app access in Instagram or Meta settings
  • Delete or edit reply rules and stop using the Service
  • Contact us to request deletion of stored account data

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to the operator of this PulseReply instance through the contact method associated with the Meta developer app that links here.
